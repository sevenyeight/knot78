78 KNOTS

Essential ties for the freshwater angler

01 Improved Clinch Knot
Lure / Hook Easy
+

How to Tie

  1. Thread 6 inches of line through the hook or lure eye.
  2. Double back and wrap the tag end around the main line 5–6 times.
  3. Pass tag end through the small loop just above the eye.
  4. Pass tag end through the big loop just formed.
  5. Wet, pull tight, and trim.
02 Palomar Knot
Lure / Hook Easy
+

How to Tie

  1. Double 6 inches of line and pass the loop through the hook eye.
  2. Tie a loose overhand knot with the doubled line.
  3. Pass the loop over the hook or lure entirely.
  4. Wet and pull both ends to tighten.
