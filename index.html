Essential Connections
Improved Clinch Knot
Lures & Swivels • Simple • 95% Strength
Pass line through the eye of the hook.
Wrap tag end around standing line 5-7 times.
Thread end through the small loop near the eye, then back through the large loop created.
Lubricate and pull tight.
Palomar Knot
All Purpose • Simple • 98% Strength
Double 6 inches of line and pass the loop through the hook eye.
Tie a loose overhand knot with the loop.
Pass the hook through the loop.
Pull both ends to tighten.
Specialized Rigging
Texas Rig (Weedless)
Heavy Cover • Advanced • Snag-Free
Thread hook tip through the top of the lure (1/4 inch).
Rotate hook so point faces back toward lure body.
Bury the hook point slightly inside the plastic so it is not exposed.
