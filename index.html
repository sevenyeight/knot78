78 KNOTS

Common & Simple
Improved Clinch Knot
Use: Lures & Swivels | Strength: High
Thread the line through the eye of the hook.
Wrap the tag end around the standing line 5-7 times.
Thread the end through the small loop next to the eye, then back through the large loop created.
Palomar Knot
Use: Direct-to-line | Strength: Maximum
Double about 6 inches of line and pass the loop through the hook eye.
Tie a loose overhand knot with the loop.
Pass the hook through the loop and pull both ends to tighten.
Specialized Rigging
Texas Rig (Weedless)
Use: Heavy Cover / "Underwater Trees" | Difficulty: Advanced
Thread the hook tip through the top of the lure and pull it through about 1/4 inch.
Rotate the hook so the point faces back toward the lure body.
Bury the hook point slightly inside the plastic so it is not exposed.
