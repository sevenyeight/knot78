Knots & Rigging

Essential ties for the freshwater angler

🎣
01 Improved Clinch Knot
Lure / Hook Easy
+

How to Tie

  1. Thread 6 inches of line through the hook or lure eye.
  2. Double the line back and wrap the tag end around the main line 5–6 times.
  3. Pass the tag end back through the small loop just above the eye.
  4. Now pass the tag end through the big loop you just formed.
  5. Wet the knot, pull the main line to cinch tight. Trim tag.
Pro Tip Wetting before cinching prevents heat friction from weakening the line. Always wet mono and fluoro knots.
02 Palomar Knot
Lure / Hook Easy
+

How to Tie

  1. Double 6 inches of line and pass the loop through the hook eye.
  2. Tie a loose overhand knot with the doubled line.
  3. Pass the loop over the hook or lure entirely.
  4. Pull the loop up above the overhand knot.
  5. Wet and pull both ends to tighten. Trim tag ends.
Best For Kastmasters, spinners, and treble-hooked lures. One of the strongest knots — retains ~95% of line strength.
03 Uni Knot
Hook / Leader / Braid Easy
+

How to Tie

  1. Run line through the eye and double back, forming a loop alongside the main line.
  2. Wrap the tag end through the loop and around both lines 6 times (4 for braid).
  3. Pull the tag end to tighten the coils down.
  4. Slide the knot down to the eye by pulling the main line.
  5. Wet and cinch firmly. Trim close.
Versatile Knot Works with mono, fluoro, and braid. Two Uni knots back-to-back make the Double Uni for joining lines.
04 Double Uni Knot
Braid-to-Leader Medium
+

How to Tie

  1. Overlap braid and leader ends by 6 inches, facing opposite directions.
  2. Tie a Uni knot with the braid around the leader — 6 wraps.
  3. Tie a second Uni knot with the leader around the braid — 4 wraps.
  4. Wet both knots, then pull the main lines in opposite directions.
  5. Slide the two knots together until snug. Trim tails.
Use This For Attaching 12–20 lb fluoro leader to your braid main line. The #1 knot for spin fishing with lures.
05 Blood Knot
Mono-to-Mono Join Hard
+

How to Tie

  1. Overlap two lines by 8 inches, facing opposite ways.
  2. Wrap line A around line B five times, then hold the center gap open.
  3. Wrap line B around line A five times in the opposite direction.
  4. Pass both tag ends through the center gap in opposite directions.
  5. Wet thoroughly, pull both main lines simultaneously to tighten.
Note Excellent for joining similar-diameter mono lines. Best for building leaders. Use Double Uni if you're newer to tying knots — it's easier.
06 Surgeon's Loop
Loop-to-Loop Easy
+

How to Tie

  1. Double the last 4 inches of your line to form a loop.
  2. Make an overhand knot with the doubled section, leaving a loop.
  3. Pass the loop through the overhand one more time (double overhand).
  4. Wet and pull the main line and tag ends to tighten.
  5. Trim tag end close to the knot.
Loop-to-Loop Connection Create a loop at both line ends to quickly swap leaders. Pass one loop through the other, then through itself — "square knot" style.
07 Non-Slip Loop Knot
Hard Lure Action Medium
+

How to Tie

  1. Tie an overhand knot 6 inches from the tag end — don't close it fully.
  2. Thread the tag end through the lure eye, then back through the overhand knot.
  3. Wrap the tag end around the main line 3 times.
  4. Pass the tag end back through the overhand knot loop.
  5. Wet, tighten slowly, trim tag.
Why Use This The loop lets spoons and crankbaits swing freely for maximum natural action — better than a clinch knot for moving lures.

Common Rigging Setups

🥄
Lure Direct Rig
Main line straight to lure. Best for open water casting with Kastmasters, Rooster Tails, or spoons. No added weight.
Knots: Palomar or Improved Clinch
🧵
Braid + Fluoro Leader
10–20 lb braid main line joined to 8–15 lb fluorocarbon leader (18–36 in). Braid gives sensitivity and no stretch; fluoro is invisible underwater.
Knots: Double Uni (join) + Palomar (terminal)
⚖️
Split Shot Rig
Crimp a split shot 12–18 inches above your hook or small lure. Great for lightweight lures in current or deeper water.
Knots: Improved Clinch or Uni
🔗
Slip Sinker (Carolina)
Egg or bullet sinker slides on main line, stopped by a bead and swivel. Leader runs to hook or lure. Fish can pick up bait without feeling weight.
Knots: Palomar (swivel) + Improved Clinch (hook)
🪝
Drop Shot Rig
Hook tied mid-line with tag hanging below to a small weight. Holds bait or soft plastic at a precise depth. Great for clear reservoirs.
Knots: Palomar (leave long tag for weight)
🔄
Loop-to-Loop Leader System
Surgeon's loop at the end of main line and top of pre-rigged leader. Swap leaders in seconds without retying. Great for quick lure changes on the water.
Knots: Surgeon's Loop (both ends)
